About ChordRush

ChordRush is a free piano chord trainer for MIDI keyboards — a chord name appears and you play it against the clock, drilling triads, 7ths, and inversions until they're automatic.

Why it exists

I'm teaching myself piano and kept running into the same thing: I knew my chords fine, I just couldn't play them fast. I'd see a chord name and sit there finding it one note at a time. More theory wasn't going to fix that. I just needed to do it over and over until my hands stopped thinking about it.

I went looking for something that would just drill that on a real keyboard and didn't really find one, so I made this. A chord shows up, you play it, you get the next one. It's a side project and it's rough in spots, but it's genuinely made me faster, which is about the only reason I keep working on it.

What you need to play

  • A MIDI keyboard connected to your computer (USB or Bluetooth).
  • Chrome or Edge on desktop — ChordRush reads your playing through the Web MIDI API, which Safari, Firefox, and phones don't support yet.

No keyboard handy? You can still look around, but the drills need live MIDI input to score what you play.

The three ways to play

  • Time Trials — race the clock through a set of chords; your best times land on the leaderboard.
  • Practice — a pressure-free warmup board: play each shape to light it up.
  • Leaderboard — see the fastest players on each chord set. Log in and your dashboard tracks your speed per chord over time.
